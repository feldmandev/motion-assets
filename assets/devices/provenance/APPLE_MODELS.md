# Apple iPhone drawings

38 models, 162 finish variants. Checked October 8, 2026.

These are manually specified SVG drawings from Apple's rear product images, not vendor-app extractions. Slabs share the collection's deliberately wider 30 × 50 footprint and rounded corners. Mini, Plus and Max variants reuse their family camera layout; these icons do not represent physical size. Finish hues are representative, rendered with the shared Pixel-style gradients. The Apple mark is extracted from the official Apple navigation SVG.

Air, XR, XS Max, mini, Plus, Pro Max, 16e, 17e, and the 2020/2022 SE generations are included. iPhone Duo uses its official announced name and closed rear design, with a wider body. It is marked provisional until its announced October 23 availability. There is no base iPhone 18 design in Apple's current compare catalog; none has been invented.

Primary rear images and finish names: [Identify your iPhone model](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108044). Current lineup: [Apple compare](https://www.apple.com/iphone/compare/). Air: [Apple product page](https://www.apple.com/iphone-air/). Duo: [Apple product page](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/).

Camera-island correction: stronger finish-tinted gradients across Apple camera housings; 17/18 Pro plateaus retain about 2.8 lens diameters of height, with symmetric lens clearance. Air and the Pro plateaus follow the body inset at their upper corners and use smaller lower corner radii. Geometry is fitted to the official rear images while preserving the shared stylized body.

Camera-spacing correction: 25 compact-island and capsule layouts now leave at least 0.04 body widths between each camera lens and the rounded island boundary. This includes the 11/12 families, 13-16 Pro families, diagonal dual-camera families, 16/17 capsules and Duo. The Air and 17/18 Pro plateaus retain their previously corrected spacing.
