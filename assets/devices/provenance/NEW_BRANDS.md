# Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO, HONOR and Motorola additions

Checked 2026-10-08. These are **drawn reference icons**, not SVGs extracted from vendor apps. The additions cover selected flagship generations and tablet families from 2020 onward, plus recent Redmi Note, POCO X, HONOR number-series and Moto G models. They do not represent every regional release or storage/connectivity SKU.

The drawing deliberately uses the wider Pixel-like silhouette, rounded corners, simplified camera glass and stronger gradients requested for this collection. Camera islands, lens positions and major rear features follow official product renders or hardware diagrams. Colors are representative interpretations of the named finishes, not color-calibrated samples.

Xiaomi 18 Pro and Pro Max are China-market models. Motorola Razr 40/50/60 names use their international names; the 2026 US Razr names retain their year to distinguish them. Moto Pad 60 Neo combines the shared rear design of its Bronze Green cellular and Malibu Blue Wi-Fi versions.

Motorola Signature 27 is omitted for now: the official teaser inspected shows the rear material and logo but not the camera layout. No camera arrangement was invented from that teaser.

The Xiaomi follow-up adds 17 Pro / Pro Max, 14 Pro, 15 Pro / 15S Pro, 17 Max, Mi 10 Ultra, Mi 11 Pro, MIX 4 / Flip / Fold 4, Pad 7 Ultra / 7S Pro 12.5 and Pad 9 / 9 Pro Max. China-market Pro names are labeled in the gallery. Pad 9 Pro and 18 Fold remain omitted because the inspected official page assets did not supply a usable rear reference.

Official source links per model are below. Retained render and diagram files are in `../tmp/ref5/new_brand_refs/`.

Flip phones show the unfolded rear: the 5G camera sits above its black cover screen, and the 40 uses a single black camera/display strip. The fold seam stays centered. The unrelated `hardware_back_6200_2.png` image embedded in the Razr 40 support page was excluded.

Mi 10 Ultra and Mi 11 Ultra were corrected on 2026-10-08 using the linked official rear renders: Mi 10 Ultra retains its silver periscope head and three lower camera blobs; Mi 11 Ultra uses a black rear display, a vertical flash beside its two upper cameras, and the periscope below the left camera. Camera modules retain simplified gradients without lens internals.

The Mi 10 / 11 Ultra camera plates use contrasting dark charcoal Pixel-style gradients to keep their dark camera blobs distinct. Mi 10 Ultra has a short, wide silver periscope cap and a horizontal rectangular opening. These are deliberate style simplifications of the linked renders.
