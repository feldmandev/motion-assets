The active collection uses `vendor/phones/` and `vendor/tablets/` for Pixel, Samsung, Nothing, OnePlus, OPPO, Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO, HONOR, Motorola and Apple. Each color has one current device SVG. Nothing currently has no tablet models.

- Pixel icons are extracted Pixel Tips artwork, except the drawn Pixel 6 family, the documented Pro/XL aliases and the adjusted Pixel 8 Pro camera capsules.
- Original Pixel and Nothing extraction files, source lists and Pixel alias metadata are preserved in `../device_icons_archive/vendor_extractions/`.
- Samsung, Nothing, OnePlus, OPPO, Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO, HONOR, Motorola and Apple model icons are drawn from the retained official-render references. Previous per-vendor drawing notes are archived with the old layout.
- Generic system icons, notification/adaptive-app variants, duplicate series exports and old vendor notes are in `../device_icons_archive/legacy_layout_20261007/`.

The Pixel 8 Pro capsule adjustment is generated by `tmp/ref5/fix_pixel_8_pro_render.py`. The model grid and standalone HTML gallery are rebuilt by `tmp/ref5/all_phones_grid.py` and `tmp/ref5/build_gallery.py`.

Pixel 6, Pixel 6 Pro and Pixel 6a are drawn by `tmp/ref5/generate_pixel_6.py`: three launch finishes each, with two-tone backs and simplified black camera glass. Their shapes and representative colors follow Google's [Pixel 6 color references](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/pixel-art-how-designers-created-new-pixel-6-colors/) and [Pixel 6a rear renders](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/pixel-6a-io-2022/). The G path is reused from the retained Pixel 7 Tips artwork. These nine additions are not vendor-app extractions; no Pixel 6-specific vectors were found in the retained Pixel Tips resources. Downloaded photo references are in `../tmp/ref5/new_brand_refs/pixel-6-reference-sheet.png` and `pixel-6a-reference.png`.

The older Samsung and OnePlus backfill, its stylization limits and official source links are documented in [OLDER_MODELS.md](OLDER_MODELS.md).

The five new vendor collections, coverage limits and official reference links are documented in [NEW_BRANDS.md](NEW_BRANDS.md).

The Apple iPhone collection, official references, finish coverage and provisional Duo are documented in [APPLE_MODELS.md](APPLE_MODELS.md).

Derived, tintable monochrome phone exports and regeneration instructions are documented in [MONOCHROME.md](MONOCHROME.md).

Camera-clearance corrections are checked by `tmp/ref5/audit_camera_clearance.py` against curved housing boundaries and inner bevel faces. Current drawn camera layouts retain at least 0.025 body widths of clearance for separately rendered lenses; connected black glass capsules remain single shapes. Samsung Flip3/4 retain the two distinct rear boundaries: cover glass and folding hinge.

Camera-corner correction: iPhone 17 Pro/Pro Max plateau reviewed against Apple dimensional drawings (https://developer.apple.com/download/files/accessories/dimensional-drawings/iphone-17-pro-max.pdf); 18 Pro/Pro Max and Air retain their existing device layouts with matching top/bottom corner treatment. Large Razr cover-screen corners use the phone radius minus the even frame inset. S21/S22 and A36/A37/A56/A57 flash/camera clearances are checked in tmp/ref5/audit_camera_clearance.py.

Phone (3a) Lite panel and flash placement reviewed against the official rear image on https://nothing.tech/products/phone-3a-lite (https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0376/5420/0459/files/BulbT-White.png). Small chassis details are simplified for the shared icon style. Tab S6 exports omit the pen strip. Tablet monochrome variants are derived from the collection artwork.

Logo correction (2026-10-22): existing marks use finish-aware tones, retaining explicit reference colors. These are display/style estimates rather than measured physical materials. Pixel 4/4a/5-family marks follow the lower rear position; Samsung tablet wordmarks share the measured reference positions/orientations used by the 3D profiles. `tmp/ref5/logo-audit.json`, `logo-tone-verification.json` and `tablet-logo-placement-changes.json` record scope and limits. Repeatable logo-only postpasses are `tmp/ref5/complete_logo_tones.py` and `tmp/ref5/fix_tablet_logos.py`. Monochrome remains tintable.

Procedural models: `device-models/make_device_branding.py` applies profile branding and `sync_logo_palettes.py` shares matching icon finish colors through `research/device-logo-palettes.json`. Pixel marks are placed on the rear/camera leaf; Samsung Flip and TriFold marks belong to their external hinges, not the blank rear panels. Branding-only GLB refreshes preserve all non-logo position buffers and existing articulated hardware. Position/size, finish response and ink/inlay thickness remain photo estimates.

Samsung Flip cover correction (2026-10-23): Flip5/6/7 FE use a photo-fitted shallow folder contour; Flip7/8 subtract camera and flash apertures from the OLED mesh rather than relying on black overlays. Native screen UV bounds and matching SVG masks are retained. Per-export `cover-checks.json` and `research/flip-cover-independent-review.json` record geometry checks. The contours are reference estimates, not CAD.

Nothing correction (2026-10-23): Phone (2), Phone (3), Phone (3a), Phone (3a) Pro and Phone (4a) drawings were reviewed against the official rear references in `tmp/ref5/nothing-svg-review/references.json`. Their new procedural models use published chassis/display dimensions and separate reference-fitted Glyph geometry through `make_nothing_profiles.py` and `nothing_geometry.py`. `research/nothing-measurements.json` records sources and estimates; `research/nothing-verification.json` records export checks. Decorative layers, corner radii, bump depths and materials remain simplified estimates. All four Phone (3a) Lite previews decoded successfully in the gallery check; no replacement reference artwork was invented.
