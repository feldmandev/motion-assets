Pixel Tips (com.google.android.apps.tips), PixelTips.apk.
*_icon.svg = device foreground (108x108), *_app_icon.svg = adaptive icon (background + foreground,
unmasked), *_monochrome_icon / *_notification_icon = themed variants.
Colour codes are Google's own: blk, blue/blu, beg (beige/porcelain), grn, gry, pnk, ppl, slv.
